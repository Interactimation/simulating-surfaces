# Getting Started with Maya

Get Maya [FREE for students](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/overview) with .edu email address

_As we generally have a wide range of skill and experience levels with Maya, my first goal is to gauge each student's ability and facility with the program (or with self-instruction)_ 

Therefore, I give you: 

## Tutorial Assignment 1

* Create a _project_ in Maya
* In a scene, create a _Polygon Primitive Sphere_
* Sign-in and download [this texture set](https://www.poliigon.com/texture/ledger-stone-wall-texture-mixed-gray/7000) 
    * Yes you may choose another source and another texture but you need at least an
        * Image texture and a
        * Normal map 
* Using Hypershade, apply _just the image texture_ to the sphere

> This is due by class time Friday, when we will apply lighting and a normal map to the same sphere in order to show what normal maps are all about!

**LEARN:** I will demonstrate, in class, how to seek only recent videos on YouTube for self instruction, but the shorthand is: 
* **In Youtube:** Enter your search term; click filter; under upload date, chose This Year
* **In Google:** Enter your search term; Chose Videos; Under Tools find Any Time and in the dropdown chose Past year OR Custom Range (which can mean the last two years or whatever boundary dates you choose)
