## Introduction

Artists, scientists and thinkers of all kinds have tried to give pictures the power of conveying information  
Time-based media adds a dimension to what kind of information can be expressed  
Interactivity allows users to enter into the simulation and experiment with its parameters  
We'll do all these things!

## _Searchlights:_
* **Infographics**  
Their History, Dynamic and Psychology

* **Interactivity**  
How the User becomes Cause to the Effect

* **Data**  
Real World Data and how to Interpret and Express it  

------

## Learning Goals and Objectives

This course will steer by these searchlights, toward these objectives:

### Goals

* Participate in the dialogue of infographic design  
* Explore the power of time-based simulation to develop an argument  
* Add interactivity to simulation, allowing users to play with (gamify) the data

### Objectives 

* **Communication:** Learners will manage the representation of information in graphical, time-based and interactive forms  
* **Innovation:** They will find best-cases for conveying complex information, conduct audience response testing and adjust strategy accordingly 
* **Social Context:** They will consider the limitations of simulation and its power to convince (mislead)

**• 3 Creativity points** -earned by showing design thinking or emphasizing the expressive and meaningful in your approach to web design   
**• 3 Innovation points** -earned whenever a learner takes it upon themselves to incorporate technical understanding gained outside the classroom  
**• 4 Engagement points** -attendance, participation, asking and answering questions and helping others all earn these points
