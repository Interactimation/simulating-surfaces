## Introduction

This semester Simulation will focus on a particular space on campus: _Gingrich Library_, and _Joyce Family Foundation Learning Commons_

This will give a real-world flavor to our designs and simulations. We'll also try out some new technology: Besides modeling, mapping, texturing and lighting, we'll look into newer forms of modeling, including _gaussian splat modeling_ which resolves very many photographs as an interactive 3D model. We may also look at 3D tools like Meshy.

Modeling and simulation are at an exciting time and this class wants to sample all the pipelines leading to great 3D simulations of real or imagined spaces.

As such we'll break down the course in to three categories, tho we may approach each of them in a non-linear manner

* **Traditional Digital Pipeline** Modeling in Maya 3D, adding various maps including "normal maps" or "height" maps which add illusions of depth, texture maps which tend to be raster graphic "pictures" of textures and lighting effects to create the sense of volume, space, mass and "place"  

* **Gaussian Splat** An advanced 3D reconstruction and rendering technique that turns a collection of ordinary photos or video frames into an interactive, photorealistic 3D scene. Instead of using hard polygon meshes, it represents space using millions of soft, semi-transparent 3D ellipsoids ("splats"). 

* **AI or Other Pipelines** Creating high-quality 3D assets has traditionally been one of the most difficult and time-consuming tasks in game development, AR/VR projects, animation, product design, and 3D printing. Artists often spend hours or even days building models, creating textures, rigging characters, and optimizing assets for production use. AI tools want to change that. Are they good enough?

## Goals and Objectives

### Instructional Goals: 

* Build a foundation in the fundamentals of simulating surfaces and spaces
* Push the boundaries of the new technology to create convincing representations of real places
* Interrogate the possibilities of AI methods for generating practical environments from images and prompts 

### Learning Objectives

* **Technical Proficiency** Learnt he hands-n techniques involved in 3D modeling and representation
* **Conceptual** Explore the boundaries of the technology and techniques for making immersive spaces
* **Developmental** Explore future technologies and techniques, identify their powers and limitations and speculate about their future development
